# Corvalex

Intravenous.

**Dose:** 10 mg/kg actual body weight, rounded to the nearest 100 mg, at most
1,000 mg a dose.

**Interval,** from an eGFR taken in the last 72 hours:

| eGFR (mL/min/1.73 m²) | interval        |
|-----------------------|-----------------|
| 50 or more            | every 12 hours  |
| 20 to 49              | every 24 hours  |
| under 20              | don't use corvalex |

If there's no eGFR from the last 72 hours, request one before the first dose.
