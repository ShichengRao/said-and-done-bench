# Nevarocin

Intravenous. Added to the formulary by the Drug and Therapeutics Committee on
14 October 2026, for infections resistant to first-line treatment, on the
infection team's advice.

Nevarocin is cleared by the kidneys and has a narrow therapeutic range: it
accumulates quickly when renal function falls, and accumulation is toxic.

**Loading dose:** 25 mg/kg actual body weight, rounded to the nearest 250 mg,
at most 2,500 mg.

**Maintenance dose:** 15 mg/kg actual body weight, rounded to the nearest
250 mg, starting one interval after the loading dose. The interval depends on
renal function:

| eGFR (mL/min/1.73 m²) | interval                                              |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| 60 or more            | every 12 hours                                        |
| 30 to 59              | every 24 hours                                        |
| 15 to 29              | every 48 hours                                        |
| under 15              | don't use nevarocin; discuss with the infection team  |

**Renal function:** dose from an eGFR taken in the last 48 hours. Renal
function changes quickly in the patients who get nevarocin, and an older
result isn't safe to dose from. If there's no eGFR from the last 48 hours,
don't calculate a dose, the loading dose included: request an urgent eGFR,
and the prescriber decides whether to give anything before the result is
back.
