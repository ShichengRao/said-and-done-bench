# Destination protocol: stroke alerts

Corran County EMS, medical director's protocol 4.2, revision 7 (1 March 2026).

1. **Suspected large vessel occlusion.** A stroke alert with a LAMS of 4 or
   more, last known well within 24 hours, goes to the nearest
   thrombectomy-capable centre within 60 minutes' drive.
2. **Other stroke alerts**, and suspected large vessel occlusions with no
   thrombectomy-capable centre eligible under rule 1, go to the nearest stroke
   centre (primary or thrombectomy-capable) within 30 minutes' drive.
3. **Diversion.** A hospital that the regional status board shows on ED
   diversion is not eligible for any ambulance patient. A hospital on stroke
   diversion is not eligible for a stroke alert. The status board is the
   source of truth; crews don't phone hospitals to ask.
4. **No eligible hospital.** If no hospital is eligible under rules 1 to 3,
   the crew calls Medical Control (on-line medical direction), which names the
   destination. Only Medical Control can override a diversion.

Drive times come from the routing service, from the unit's grid cell.
