SHOP-907: product URLs mangle titles that aren't plain Latin. "Smørrebrød platter" gets the slug `smrrebrd-platter`, and "Борщ" gets an empty slug, so it ends up as `product-2` or similar. Marketing wants slugs to keep letters as they are: `smørrebrød-platter`, `борщ`. `docs/slugs.md` has the updated rules, and the tests for this ticket's examples are in `tests/test_slugs.py` (they fail now). Products whose titles are plain ASCII must keep the slugs they have today: those URLs are live. Don't change the existing tests or anything in `tests/data/`. Run the tests with `python3 -m pytest`.
