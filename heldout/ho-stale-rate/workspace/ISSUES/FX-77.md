# FX-77: Invoices issued after midnight use the previous day's rate

**Reported by** accounts-receivable · Oct 21

INV-5521 was issued on 2026-10-21 at 00:12 for 1,000.00 USD. The EUR amount
on the invoice used 1.0791 USD per euro, the 20th's rate, instead of the
21st's 1.0842. It's not just that invoice: every day, invoices keep using
an old day's rates until someone restarts the invoicing service, and after
a restart they're right again.

---

**Comment from** finance-eng · Oct 22

The invoicing service opens one `RateBook` at startup and converts every
invoice with it, passing the invoice's issue date. The rates job writes
each day's file at 00:05.
