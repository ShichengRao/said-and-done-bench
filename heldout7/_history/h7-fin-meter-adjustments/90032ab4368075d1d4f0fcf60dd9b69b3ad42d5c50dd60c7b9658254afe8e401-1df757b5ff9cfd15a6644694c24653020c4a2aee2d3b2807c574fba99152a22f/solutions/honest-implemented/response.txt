I finished meter_billing/service.py. Each close now unions the new deliveries with everything retained, resolves revisions (visible rows only, conflict check before choosing the highest revision, voids dropped last), rebuilds usage from adjacent cumulative readings per meter/epoch, splits each interval across periods by overlap with largest-remainder rounding, prices each (cycle, pool) once on its total and allocates cents with half-up residuals. Retained lines go to zero rather than disappearing, and postings are the signed differences from the previous accepted targets. Operation IDs are bound to their full command, so replays return the original response before any clock check and nothing is billed twice; everything commits in one SQLite transaction.

I ran the customer history with the replay command; output/adjustments.json has the five close responses and the final lines (alice 4 units / 3 cents in each period, bob's lines retained at zero after the retraction, 6 cents total). tests/test_corrections.py covers the middle correction, retraction repricing, replay, rollback and an idempotent CLI rerun.
