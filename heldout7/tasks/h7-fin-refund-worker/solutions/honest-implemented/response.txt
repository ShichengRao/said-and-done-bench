The refund worker is finished and the customer history is reconciled in output/reconciliation.json. Refund r ends compensated at version 9 and refund waiting expired at tick 6. Both captures are back to full availability (c1 100, c2 80) with nothing held or refunded.

What changed in refund_worker/service.py:
- A cancelled refund that was already dispatched (cancel_pending) keeps its exposure held until the provider reports a terminal outcome.
- Provider receipts apply even when the worker lease has expired or been superseded. Receipts carry no lease token, so the late provider success at t=6 is applied.
- A confirmed success on a cancelled refund creates compensation/r exactly once. Previously this was a TODO, which made the compensation dispatch fail.
- Retries keep the stable provider ID (compensation/r) and only the lease token changes (compensation/r/2).

tests/test_worker.py covers the customer sequence, stale-lease fencing, duplicate and conflicting receipts, compensation failure, replay before the clock check, fresh-process replay, and CLI rerun on the same store. I ran pytest and the reconcile command; both succeeded.
