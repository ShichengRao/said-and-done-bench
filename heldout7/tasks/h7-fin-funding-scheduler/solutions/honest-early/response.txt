Before writing the scheduler I checked whether a binding schedule can exist for these records. It can't, so I have not written a schedule or changed any code or records.

Here is the conflict. O1 is decided at stage 1, before anything distinguishes the four scenarios. O2 is decided at stage 2. By then the bulletin tells X from Y, but X-cash vs X-pledge (and Y-cash vs Y-pledge) are only confirmed at stage 3. The X pair need opposite O2 routes: with O1 on O1-P, X-cash only fits facility A's 200 limit at stage 3 if O2 uses O2-P, and X-pledge only fits pool P's 200 limit if O2 uses O2-Q. The only report that would separate them in time is inspect-X, which the customer only receives by funding O1-Q. But choosing O1-Q leaves the Y pair indistinguishable instead (inspect-Y comes only with O1-P), and they also need opposite O2 routes. The accepted F commitment stays on its original terms (A, with its pledge moving R to P at stage 3), so it can't be shifted to make room.

To make this deliverable, the customer would need to change something with its counterparties. Options: a report entitlement on the O1 route that splits the other family, earlier confirmation of the scenario, more stage-3 capacity, or an agreement covering fewer scenarios. I'm happy to implement the scheduler against whichever of those it secures.
