## Explicit finite bounds

Alias replay has at most three shards and six transactions, with at most `6^3 = 216` cut vectors. Attestation has at most three chains of four events, bounding raw interleavings by 34,650, and at most 64 proposal subsets. Owner planning has at most three phases, four owners, six groups and eight local paths per group, with the full product explicitly bounded by 4,096 even after proposals; at most 48 revision entries precede planning. Regional placement has at most four datasets, six eligible templates each, four scenarios and five proposals, giving at most 1,296 placement products.

Service v3 permits six requests, three starts, two routes per request and six locally authorized choices per request, so at most 46,656 full assignments and 32 owner subsets are needed (20,736 in the supplied case). Its evidence is bounded by 32 credentials, eight pools, 16 distinct attempts and 24 distinct receipts after identical replay collapse. Export v3 has one to five people, at most 16 aliases, 16 consent rows, four prior publications, three packages with two counts each, and twelve possible current datasets: at most 1,024 old/current pairs and 531,441 complete policy assignments. Ticket projection has at most six post-base epochs, three fragments per epoch and 30 trace commands.

These public bounds permit exhaustive algorithms without external solvers. Ordering and tie-breaks select reproducible witnesses; they do not supply the intended difficulty.
