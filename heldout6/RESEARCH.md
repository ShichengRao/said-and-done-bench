# Public workflow grounding

Refreshed October 31, 2026 before implementation. The source limitations in [Round 4 research](../heldout4/RESEARCH.md) and [Round 5 research](../heldout5/RESEARCH.md) still apply. All customer names, datasets, policies, and operational scenarios in this collection are fictional.

[Reflection's launch post](https://reflection.ai/blog/beam-is-available) and [Mirror overview](https://developers.reflection.ai/mirror-overview) describe repository work, code changes, commands, review, and agent integrations. They ground software-maintenance workflows without establishing that named customers run these exact applications.

The [Responsible Use Guide](https://developers.reflection.ai/responsible-use-guide) discusses access scope, approval boundaries, customer-data systems, and operational safeguards. Data migration, authorization reconstruction, audit evidence, and controlled release exercises are engineering extrapolations from those concerns. All applicable rules are supplied within each offline task.

The [Kubernetes serving documentation](https://developers.reflection.ai/serving-kubernetes) grounds production configuration, storage, workers, and deployments. It supports the operations workflows, without implying that these fictional recovery and maintenance incidents occurred.

The Andon Labs section of the [Safety Technical Report](https://reflection.ai/beam-safety-report) describes a vending-business deployment with inventory, pricing, procurement, delivery, spending limits, and human approval for transfers. That deployment grounds procurement and accounting exercises. Other billing, payout, credit, and revenue workflows remain fictional extensions, not documented customer case studies.

Public evaluation results are not used to choose model-specific weaknesses. The tasks test general engineering and diagnosis under explicit contracts, and require no external research or internet access by the tested agent. Task metadata names the applicable workflow and source title without URLs, as required by the brief.
