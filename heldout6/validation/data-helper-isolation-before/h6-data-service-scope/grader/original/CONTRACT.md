# Delegated batch admission from reservation evidence

Aster must admit an indivisible reconciliation batch through alternative approved delegated service routes. Different chains share both tenant allowances and delegated service allowances; those charges remain spent across later start slots. The shared allowance already has committed calls and outstanding reservations. Client retries, timeouts and rejected calls do not by themselves establish whether allowance was spent or released. Reconstruct the authoritative reservation state at the supplied cutoff, then find a whole-call route/start assignment under that state and exact credential/request windows. This is an offline admission audit, not permission to execute calls.

## Current authority and candidate calls

`schedule` contains `{as_of,slots,workers,budgets,credentials,requests,routes}`. Times are integers. Every candidate start in slots is >=as_of. workers maps each stringified start to its capacity; this is START-slot capacity charged once, not a concurrency limit. budgets maps signed shared-pool IDs to total allowance for this batch period. There is no quota reset during the supplied horizon. Capacities/allowances are nonnegative integers.

A credential is `{id,parent,scopes,start,end,pool}`. parent names another credential or is null. Credential definitions are immutable and parent links acyclic. Different credentials may share a pool. A request is `{id,scopes,units,duration,earliest,latest,leaves}` with positive integer units and duration. latest is its COMPLETION deadline. A route is `{id,request,credentials:[ordered IDs]}`. It must begin at a root, follow exact parent links, end at an approved leaf and contain every required scope at every credential. No other chain is an approved route.

A local option at t requires earliest<=t and t+duration<=latest, and every credential requires start<=t and t+duration<=end. Completion exactly at a deadline/credential end is allowed; completion one unit later is not. Each admitted call spends its units once from EACH DISTINCT pool anywhere in its route and from the chosen worker start. The allowance is reserved at start and permanently spent for the rest of this batch; call completion does not return it. Repeated pool IDs on distinct credentials charge once. Indivisible calls cannot split units across routes or starts.

## Immutable attempted calls and receipts

`ledger` contains `{attempts,receipts}`. An attempt is `{id,key,at,credentials,units,transport}`. key is an idempotency key in this batch; units is positive; credentials is the original ordered credential chain offered by that attempt. transport is `response`, `timeout`, or `error`. Those transport observations never debit or release allowance. A later retry may offer a different chain or units and be rejected; it cannot rewrite an earlier accepted reservation. Historical accepted receipts are authoritative, so do not revalidate the old call against today's candidate scope/window rules.

Receipts are:

* `{id,kind:'reserve',at,reservation,attempt,expires}`: acknowledges the binding of a reservation to the named ORIGINAL accepted attempt and its key, units and distinct ancestor pools. The first acknowledgment must precede expires. Its hold occupies allowance on the half-open interval from acknowledgment to expires. Later reserve acknowledgments for the same reservation must repeat that exact accepted attempt and expiry; they do not charge again, even after expiry or settlement.
* `{id,kind:'commit',at,reservation}`: commits the previously acknowledged hold permanently for this batch. The first commit must occur strictly before expires. Repeated commit acknowledgments are harmless, including those delivered after expires.
* `{id,kind:'release',at,reservation}`: releases the previously acknowledged hold. Repeated releases are harmless. A release after automatic expiry is also harmless. A reservation cannot have both commit and release receipts.
* `{id,kind:'reject',at,attempt}`: rejects this attempt only. It does not undo any reservation/commit associated with that attempt's key.

Receipt at is its authoritative effective time, not arrival order. Interpret visible receipts in `(at,id)` order; at<=as_of is visible and later receipts have no state effect at this cutoff. A visible reserve/reject receipt must not precede its attempt. A visible terminal receipt must follow a visible reserve in that ordering. All rows may be physically unordered. Identical replays with the same attempt ID or receipt ID collapse to one record; a changed payload under the same ID raises ValueError. Reservation IDs and idempotency keys are different namespaces. One key can bind only one reservation within this batch. Reusing a reservation with another accepted attempt/expiry, reusing a key for another reservation, contradictory terminals, a first commit at or after expiry, or terminal-before-reserve raises ValueError. No automatic new generation is inferred from a retry.

Validate duplicate credential/route IDs, unknown credential parent/pool/route/attempt-chain IDs, unknown receipt attempt/reservation references, and conflicting record replays across ALL rows, including later rows. These raise ValueError. Temporal/binding/terminal consistency applies to visible receipts. All other shapes, types and references are guaranteed valid. Every historical attempted chain starts at a root and follows exact parent links; the historical visible committed/held units fit the supplied allowance. Different namespaces may reuse names. Future rows must have valid references even when invisible.

At the cutoff, a reservation is committed, released, held, or expired. With no terminal receipt it is expired when expires<=as_of; otherwise held. A committed reservation stays charged after its expiry. For each candidate start t, available allowance equals signed allowance minus committed units minus units of held reservations with t<expires. Rejected/never-acknowledged attempts cost nothing. Each reservation's charge always uses its original accepted attempt, never the newest attempt with that key. Receipts at as_of are included; a held reservation expiring exactly at a candidate start no longer holds its units there.

## Finite audit and owner requests

Bounds: at most six requests, three starts, two routes per request, six locally authorized options per request, thirty-two credentials, eight pools, sixteen distinct attempts, twenty-four distinct receipts after identical replay collapse, and five owner proposals. Requests/streams of evidence may be empty. Thus at most6^6 =46,656 complete assignments and32 proposal subsets need consideration. No external optimizer is required.

`proposals` contain unique IDs and positive integer cost/units. A budget proposal `{id,cost,kind:'budget',pool,units}` adds allowance to that shared pool at every start. A worker proposal `{id,cost,kind:'worker',slot,units}` adds capacity at one supplied start. These are hypothetical owner approvals, not current authority; they do not alter historical receipts, credential windows or request deadlines.

Return exactly `{ready,reservation_state,availability,route_options,assignment,obstruction,owner_request,blocker_codes}`. reservation_state maps every visibly acknowledged reservation ID to `{key,attempt,units,pools,expires,state}` with sorted distinct pools and its cutoff state. availability maps every pool to `{slot_string:remaining_units}` under current historical evidence. route_options maps every request to `{route,slot,pools}` options sorted by route ID then numeric slot; it applies local credential/scope/time rules, not shared quotas or workers.

For any selection of new calls, its cumulative units in each pool at EACH supplied start t must not exceed availability[p][str(t)]; count every selected call starting at or before t. Worker use at a start must fit that start's capacity. assignment is the least complete feasible `{request_id:{route,slot}}` or null, comparing `(route_id,slot)` sequences in sorted request-ID order. ready is true iff it exists. If infeasible, obstruction is the smallest subset still infeasible when all other new requests are removed but historical charges are retained; break ties lexically on sorted request IDs. Otherwise obstruction is [].

owner_request considers every proposal subset and selects least total cost, then fewest proposals, then sorted proposal IDs lexically. Return `{proposal_ids,cost,assignment}` with its least full assignment, or null if none works. An already feasible base uses the empty subset at cost0. Keep availability/base assignment/obstruction about CURRENT authority. blocker_codes is [] when ready, otherwise [`batch_delegation_budget_required`]. Explain the actual missing allowance or worker approval, without changing observations or executing calls.
