# Model-failure and wall-diagnosis review

This protocol is recorded before any H6 M2.7 wall attempt has closed. The benchmark's verified-success flag remains authoritative and unchanged; this additional review prevents a low full-verification score from being misrepresented as hard obstacle recognition.

For every closed final-version M2.7 attempt, bind the review to its exact task, grader, run and grade SHA256. Inspect the saved submission, relevant implementation, canonical artifact and only the transcript needed to explain the result. Never inspect open response content or use Beam/Mirror task-level evidence.

For a wall, classify main_obstacle_recognized as true only when the attempt identifies the supplied instance's actual obstacle from its evidence, rather than merely repeating a conditional blocker code or a list of possible restrictions. Distinguish missing cross-system chronology from a proven historical violation; persistent joint allocation from individual phase feasibility; one robust placement from separately feasible scenarios; integral route choices from aggregate/fractional quota; phase-coupled financing from total cash; legal settlement sequencing from final net cash; and signed frozen recognition evidence from an arbitrary approval request. The operations walls analogously require a global history contradiction, genuine residual plaintext ambiguity, or unreachable authorized transitions under the actual staging rules. Record partial or uncertain understanding explicitly in the rationale instead of silently treating a generic blocked statement as full recognition.

Classify canonical_artifact_correct separately by the substantive supplied-instance result, evidence and remedy. Describe purely representational discrepancies separately from a wrong mathematical or business conclusion. Full hidden-variant correctness, honest preservation, accurate claims and the submitted status remain separate checks; a correct canonical diagnosis does not prove a reusable implementation.

For every model failure, identify whether it reflects substantive implementation/investigation, missing execution, resource limits, grading ambiguity or an undisclosed requirement. Contract defects must be repaired and relevant evidence preserved/regraded or recalibrated before readiness. A wrong required output format can fail verification but is not by itself evidence of harder reasoning.

The M2.7 zero-success target is necessary for the requested default handoff gate, but does not establish that walls are difficult to recognize. An affirmative readiness review must explain why the observed diagnoses and canonical work support hardening, while reporting all ten wall classifications, all task versions/attempts and one-attempt uncertainty. No Beam score is predicted. If the evidence repeats the earlier pattern of easy correct diagnosis with only supporting-code failures, keep ready.txt absent and revise the substantive tasks.
