# Workflow grounding for the redesign

The replacement tasks continue the twenty fictional workflows researched before Round 4. The source inventory and limitations are preserved in [Round 4 research](../heldout4/RESEARCH.md). Public materials were refreshed on October 30, 2026 before replacement authoring.

[Reflection's launch post](https://reflection.ai/blog/beam-is-available) describes coding, reasoning, and repository-agent use. The [Responsible Use Guide](https://developers.reflection.ai/responsible-use-guide) grounds scoped access, human authorization, safeguards, and evaluation of deployed systems. The [Kubernetes serving documentation](https://developers.reflection.ai/serving-kubernetes) describes on-premises serving, shared storage, workers, and deployment configuration. These support fictional enterprise data, release, and operational workflows; they do not substantiate a named customer's use of these exact benchmark applications.

The [Safety Technical Report](https://reflection.ai/beam-safety-report) includes the Andon Labs vending pilot used to ground the procurement workflow. A pilot is not represented here as a general enterprise production deployment. Finance, billing, and customer migrations remain fictional engineering extrapolations rather than named customer case studies. All data and operational policies are invented and complete for each exercise.

Individual task metadata names the applicable workflow and source title without embedding external requirements in the agent's workspace. No task requires internet access or knowledge of a real customer's internal systems.
