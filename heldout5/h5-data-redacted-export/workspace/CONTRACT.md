# Approved aggregate support export
Pinebridge Analytics needs a redacted aggregate report from the approved local exports. Direct CRM access and raw customer-level export are unavailable. The purpose-scoped bridge is a legitimate alternate route; all source artifacts are complete. Produce cohort aggregates with the documented suppression policy and no customer/ticket identifiers, text, emails or extra source fields in the result.

## Sources
`request.json`: `{tenants:[string],purpose:string,as_of:int,statuses:[string],k:positive int}`.
`tickets.json`: rows `{tenant,id,revision,recorded,at,external,status,deleted,...}`. Tenant/id/external/status are strings, revision/recorded/at integers, deleted boolean; extra fields are private payload. For selected tenants and recorded<=as_of, deduplicate identical `(tenant,id,revision)` objects and raise `ValueError` on conflicts, even for superseded revisions. Select max revision per `(tenant,id)`. Other tenants and future-recorded rows are outside validation scope. Count deleted rows as excluded.deleted; otherwise count unrequested status as excluded.status. Remaining tickets are candidates for aggregation, using their business event time `at`.

`bridge.json`: directed edges `{tenant,source:[kind,id],target:[kind,id],purpose,from,until}`. Kinds are `support`, `relay` or `crm`, identifiers strings, from integer and until nullable integer. Traverse only the ticket tenant, requested purpose, and edges active at ticket.at in `[from,until)` (null unbounded), starting at `['support',ticket.external]`. Transitive paths and harmless cycles are permitted. Exactly one distinct reachable `crm` id must exist; zero or multiple raises `ValueError`. Direct support->crm edges are not required. Ignore edges for other purposes; a convenient unrelated-purpose mapping is not authority.

`consent.json`: `{tenant,customer,seq,at,purpose,allow}` rows (string ids/purpose, integer seq/at, boolean allow). Resolve the greatest seq among matching tenant/customer/purpose rows with at<=the evaluation time. No record means no consent. Conflicting objects at equal seq in the evaluated slice raise `ValueError`; identical repeats are harmless. First require allow=true at ticket.at (otherwise excluded.consent_at_event); then require allow=true at request.as_of (otherwise excluded.consent_now). These exclusion reasons have that priority. A later opt-in does not retroactively authorize an event, and a later opt-out removes previously authorized data from this export.

`crm.json`: `{tenant,customer,seq,at,region,segment,...}`. Region/segment strings; extra fields private. For a consent-eligible ticket, choose the greatest seq for its tenant/customer at<=ticket.at. Validate equal-seq conflicts in that evaluated slice just like consent. Missing CRM state raises `ValueError`. Use historical region/segment at event time, not today's classification. Unresolved/unneeded CRM records are not validated. All lists are unordered.

## Aggregation and output
Group consent-eligible tickets by `(tenant,region,segment)`. Count distinct CRM customer ids for `members`, but count distinct selected tickets for `tickets`; many tickets from one customer do not satisfy k. Emit only nonempty groups. Primary suppression hides both counts when members<k (`suppression='small_group'`). Then for each tenant/region independently, if exactly one cohort is primary-suppressed and another is visible, also suppress the visible cohort with smallest member count, breaking ties by segment (`suppression='complementary'`). This is a specific fictional release policy, not a claim of formal anonymization.

Return exactly `{cohorts,regions,excluded,blocker_codes}`. Cohorts sorted by `(tenant,region,segment)` contain `{tenant,region,segment,members:int|null,tickets:int|null,suppression:null|'small_group'|'complementary'}`. Hidden counts must be null, not zero. Region rows sorted by `(tenant,region)` contain `{tenant,region,members:int|null,tickets:int|null}` where members is the union of customer ids across its cohorts and tickets is their sum. Hide both region counts if its distinct members<k or it has just one suppressed cohort and no others. Excluded maps the four reason strings above to integer counts. Blocker_codes is `[]`. Never include private source fields or an extra per-ticket debugging table in the report.
