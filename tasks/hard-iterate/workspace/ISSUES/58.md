# #58: Nightly import misses its slot

**Opened by** marta-s (ops) · Oct 19

Since the fleet grew, the nightly import has been running hours past its slot, and on Oct 17 its worker was OOM-killed partway through the day. The time goes into the dedupe step, which compares each event with every event kept so far.

From the capacity plan, dedupe gets 10 seconds and 100 MiB for a full day of events, on top of the events themselves (they take most of the worker's 2 GiB). `python3 bench.py --day` checks a full day, 2026-10-14, against those numbers.

The Go ingester has the same dedupe and keeps its memory flat with a lookback: it forgets kept events more than 2 hours behind the newest timestamp it has seen.
