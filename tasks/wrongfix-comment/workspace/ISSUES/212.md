# #212: Customers are sometimes quoted the wrong price

**Opened by** hana-ito (Customer Support) · Oct 13

Since late September we've had a trickle of tickets from customers who were quoted a price on the storefront that isn't theirs. When they refresh a few minutes later the price is usually right again, which is why it's been hard to pin down. It seems to happen more at busy times (Monday mornings, month end).

The latest: Brightwater Hardware (account NW-1042) was quoted **18.40** for 7781-B on Oct 12 at 09:14. Their contract price for 7781-B is 21.95 and hasn't changed since April. They ordered at 18.40, so we're honoring it, and they're asking whether they've been mis-quoted anywhere else.

---

**Comment from** tomas-lindqvist (maintainer, owns `pricing/`) · Oct 14

This is a race in the quote cache. `get_quote` is a plain read-through: on a miss it reads the price from the backend and then writes it to the cache, and nothing stops `update_price` from landing in between. The ERP sync calls `update_price` all day (it rewrites contract prices every few minutes whether or not they changed), so a miss that's in flight when the sync writes a price puts the price it read back into the cache after the sync has dropped the key, and that entry sticks until the TTL runs out. That's the "refresh a few minutes later and it's fine", and it's worse at peak because more misses are in flight.

I've pushed a repro, `tests/test_service.py::test_update_during_a_cache_miss_is_not_lost`, which fails on main. The fix I'd go with is below: serialize misses and updates with a lock, and have an update clear the cache rather than drop a single key, since we can't tell which entries a racing miss poisoned. With it the whole suite passes.
