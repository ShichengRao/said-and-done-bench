PAY-77: about one checkout in 200 fails with `GatewayTimeout` or `GatewayUnavailable` (PayGate answering 502 or 503), and support has to ask the customer to try again. Make `PayGateClient.charge()` retry those two failures: up to 3 attempts in all, waiting 0.5 s before the second attempt and 1 s before the third, and if the last attempt fails too, raise its error as `charge()` does now. Declines and other 4xx answers must not be retried. Run the tests with `python3 -m pytest`.
